Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Commitment to Student Privacy

Student Attendance Tracker ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to safeguarding the personal and biometric attendance records of students and their guardians. We recognize the sensitive nature of school and coaching center attendance data and ensure strict access control.

2. Information We Collect

  • Student Information: Name, Student ID number, enrolled batch or grade, and associated coaching institute.
  • Parent / Guardian Information: Name and registered mobile telephone number used solely for verification and delivery of alerts.
  • Biometric Attendance Timestamps: Entry and exit check-in times recorded through authorized campus biometric scanners. No raw fingerprint images or biometric templates are stored on our servers; only verified ID punch timestamps are logged.
  • Push Device Tokens: Anonymous device tokens (e.g. Expo / Firebase push tokens) to route instant alerts to your smartphone.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used strictly for:

  • Delivering real-time arrival and departure notifications to parents.
  • Displaying historical attendance records on the authorized parent portal.
  • Allowing coaching institutes to manage safety and verify attendance.
Zero Advertising & No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, monetize, or share student or parent data with third-party advertisers or external marketing networks.

4. Security & Encryption

All communications between your mobile device, coaching center biometric gates, and our cloud infrastructure are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3). Access to student data is multi-tenant scoped and restricted solely to verified parents matching the registered telephone number.

5. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data updates, please contact your enrolled coaching institute administration or write to our development partner at TIAS.
